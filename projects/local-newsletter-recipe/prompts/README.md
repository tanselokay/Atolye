# Prompts

The real prompts the local models run. They are the craft worth copying. Each is editorial
instruction only — no servers, keys or paths. Adapt the language and voice to your own newsletter.

- `01-headline.md` — turn a source title into a faithful headline (+ few-shot pairs).
- `02-critique.md` — the short per-story critique ("AI Kritik"), two-stage: reason in English,
  then render to the target language.
- `03-cover.md` — cover-art direction: the house style (with its morning-light signature), plus
  how scenes are proposed and judged.
- `04-translation.md` — the glossary-guided English→Turkish translator: the anti-calque, register
  and sentence-discipline rules, with few-shot pairs. The heart of the Turkish quality.
- `05-shortlist.md` — the newsworthiness triage that thins the pool before a human looks.
